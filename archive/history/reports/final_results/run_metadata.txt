Dataset: corrected_v1; master_corrected_v1.parquet and depeg_episodes_corrected_v1.parquet; bar-close timestamps, new merged episode targets, causal recovery exclusion.

XGBoost uses exactly 300 trees, without early stopping. Average precision uses sklearn.average_precision_score.

False-alarm budget: 1 per observed coin-month.
Models: baseline, persistence, xgboost, random_forest
Tiers: 1, 2
Horizons (hours): 1, 6, 24
